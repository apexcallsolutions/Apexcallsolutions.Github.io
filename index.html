TCPA Compliance Policy

Apex Call Solutions · Effective November 21, 2026

1. Our Commitment

Apex Call Solutions operates in full compliance with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), the Telemarketing Sales Rule (TSR), the Truth in Caller ID Act, applicable state telemarketing laws, and Do Not Call (DNC) requirements. Compliance is a condition of participation in our network — for our own operations and for every publisher and buyer we work with.

2. Consent Requirements

  • All calls must originate from consumers who have provided prior express consent — and prior express written consent where required — to be contacted about the relevant service
  • Consent records (source, timestamp, and the disclosure shown to the consumer) must be retained by the traffic source and produced on request
  • Consumers who revoke consent must be honored immediately and suppressed from future contact

3. Do Not Call Screening

Outbound campaigns must be scrubbed against the National DNC Registry, applicable state DNC lists, and internal suppression lists before dialing, and re-scrubbed at legally required intervals.

4. Caller ID Integrity

Caller ID spoofing is strictly prohibited. Every call must display a valid, callable number that accurately identifies the calling party, consistent with STIR/SHAKEN attestation standards.

5. Prohibited Traffic

  • Robocalls or prerecorded/artificial voice calls without the required consent
  • Bot-generated, incentivized, or otherwise fabricated calls
  • Traffic sourced from scraped, purchased, or stolen personal data
  • Calls to consumers on DNC registries without an applicable exemption or consent

6. Call Recording & Disclosure

Calls may be recorded for quality and compliance. Where state law requires two-party consent, appropriate disclosures are made at the start of the call.

7. Publisher Obligations & Audits

Publishers warrant that all traffic they route through Apex is compliant, and agree to reasonable compliance audits, including production of consent records and call recordings. We may pause any campaign immediately upon a compliance concern, pending review.

8. Enforcement

Violations result in rejection of the affected calls without payment, suspension or termination of the partner account, and clawback of payments tied to non-compliant traffic.

9. Report a Concern

To report a compliance concern or request removal from contact, email apexcallsolutions.pk@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp at +92 316 3909916. Reports are reviewed promptly.
